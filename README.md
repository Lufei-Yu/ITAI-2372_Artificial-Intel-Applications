# ITAI-2372_Artificial-Intel-Applications

This repository contains my coursework, assignments, and hands-on projects for ITAI-2372: Artificial Intelligence Applications at Houston City College (HCC). Throughout this course, I explore how Artificial Intelligence is developed, applied, and integrated into real-world scenarios. The coursework covers various AI concepts and technologies, including Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Machine Learning, and AI Agents. Through practical exercises and projects, I aim to strengthen my technical skills, understand AI's capabilities and limitations, and learn to use it responsibly to solve real-world problems.


## Course Objectives

The main learning objectives of this course include:

- Understand fundamental concepts and applications of Artificial Intelligence.
- Explore different AI technologies and their real-world uses.
- Learn how AI can improve business processes and decision-making.
- Understand the capabilities and limitations of Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Explore AI agents and their potential applications.
- Evaluate ethical considerations, responsible AI practices, and potential risks.
- Develop practical skills through hands-on assignments and projects.

## Topics & Learning Areas

Throughout this course, I explore several important areas of Artificial Intelligence:

- **AI Fundamentals:** Introduction to AI concepts, history, and applications.
- **Generative AI:** Understanding AI tools that generate text, images, and other content.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Exploring tools such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini.
- **AI Agents:** Understanding how AI agents can perform tasks and support automated workflows.
- **Machine Learning:** Introduction to how machines learn from data and make predictions.
- **AI in Business:** Exploring how organizations use AI to improve productivity and operational efficiency.
- **Responsible AI:** Examining ethical challenges, bias, privacy, transparency, and the impact of AI on society.
